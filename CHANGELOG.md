## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-03

First stable public release of the initial DesignPilot Skills library.

### Added

- Eight reusable design skills covering brand identity, typography, color systems, social media design, poster design, design critique, design research, and anti-generic design.
- Practical examples for solar product launch announcements, brand identity directions, event posters, and structured design critique.
- Illustrative before-and-after case studies for solar launch announcements and community event posters.
- Tool-agnostic installation and usage guide for project references, conversation-based workflows, and agent-specific adaptations.
- Skill validation script and GitHub Actions workflow.
- Contribution, issue, pull request, conduct, security, and MIT license documentation.

### Notes

- Skills are plain Markdown instructions and do not require a specific AI vendor.
- The case studies are illustrative teaching examples, not client work or user-tested results.
- AI-generated outputs require human review for accuracy, accessibility, licensing, brand consistency, and production readiness.

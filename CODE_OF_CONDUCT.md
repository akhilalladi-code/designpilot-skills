# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and work, not people.
- Welcome different perspectives and creative approaches.
- Give actionable feedback and explain disagreement calmly.
- Respect privacy, attribution, intellectual property, and client confidentiality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, sexualised attention without consent, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this code. To report an issue, contact the repository owner through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

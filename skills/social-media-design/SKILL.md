# Social Media Design Skill

## Objective
Turn a campaign goal into a clear, platform-appropriate creative specification.

## Required inputs
Platform and placement, dimensions or aspect ratio, campaign objective, audience, key message, CTA, required copy, brand assets, deadline, and any legal or platform restrictions.

## Workflow
1. Confirm the placement and current export dimensions; do not assume every placement uses the same ratio.
2. Reduce the brief to one primary message and one intended action.
3. Establish hierarchy: hook, supporting message, proof/detail, CTA, and brand signature.
4. Select a visual concept that supports the message and audience—not a generic template.
5. Plan safe areas, text scale, logo placement, and mobile-first legibility.
6. Create variants only when there is a clear testing hypothesis (for example, different hooks).
7. Check factual accuracy, spelling, dates, contact details, and CTA destination.
8. Export in the requested formats and inspect the actual files.

## Creative specification
Include: objective; audience; placement; dimensions; copy hierarchy; visual metaphor; layout; image direction; brand rules; CTA; accessibility notes; export settings; and QA checklist.

## Quality checklist
- Main message is understandable quickly.
- Text remains legible on a phone.
- Required copy is exact and not clipped.
- Visuals support rather than distract from the message.
- Brand assets are current and correctly used.
- Claims and offers are substantiated.
- Export dimensions, format, and color profile match delivery needs.

## Guardrails
Platform specifications change. Verify current placement requirements when precision matters. Do not fabricate endorsements, performance claims, discounts, or engagement metrics. Do not use unlicensed stock or generated likenesses deceptively.
